South Florida Property & Insurance Industry Brief

July 25, 2026 | Palm Beach, Miami-Dade, Broward, Martin, St. Lucie

Today's signal: The 30-year mortgage average rose to 6.58% this week, while two targeted property-capital windows remain open. My Safe Florida Home says eligible grant applications tied to existing inspections are still being accepted even though new inspection intake is paused, and Miami-Dade's multifamily Surtax, SHIP, and HOME funding round remains open through August 14. Today's execution risk is weather: stronger afternoon storms could produce quick heavy rain and localized street flooding in poor-drainage areas. Protect active work and refresh financing and funding files before the mid-afternoon window.

TOP STORIES

1. The 30-year mortgage average rises to 6.58%

Freddie Mac reported a 6.58% national average for a 30-year fixed mortgage on July 23, up from 6.55% a week earlier and below the 6.74% reading one year ago. The 15-year average rose to 5.96% from 5.93%.

The survey is a national benchmark based on conventional conforming purchase applications, not a quote for a specific buyer or property.

Action: compare same-day lender quotes with consistent assumptions, then recalculate the complete housing cost, including insurance, flood, association dues, and assessments.

Source: Freddie Mac

2. My Safe Florida Home grants remain open while new inspections are paused

The program says grant funding remains available and it anticipates accepting grant applications through August. New inspection applications are paused. The two applications are different: an initial program inspection identifies improvements used in the later grant application.

SB 1452 established a 24-month grant-application deadline after the initial inspection and an 18-month completion period for approved improvements. It also revised attached-property eligibility and certain repeat-inspection rules. The operational pause still controls new inspection intake today.

Action: homeowners with an existing inspection should verify portal status and deadlines now. Contractors should not promise approval, reimbursement, or eligibility.

Sources: My Safe Florida Home, Florida Senate, Florida DFS

3. Miami-Dade's multifamily funding round closes August 14

Miami-Dade is accepting FY 2026 Surtax, SHIP, and HOME funding applications for multifamily rental new construction, redevelopment, and rehabilitation. The final window runs through August 14, and submissions must use ZoomGrants.

Action: applicants should work from the final RFA and score sheets and reconcile site, financing, budget, schedule, compliance, insurance, and scope before submitting.

Source: Miami-Dade Housing and Community Development

4. Property-claim mediation hearings move to August 5

Florida DFS moved hearings on proposed residential and commercial residential property-claim mediation changes from July 31 to August 5. The residential hearing is scheduled for 2 p.m. EDT and the commercial residential hearing for 3 p.m., with remote attendance.

The proposals are intended to standardize and streamline the Department's mediation programs. They are not final rules.

Action: monitor the final text, but do not miss a current claim or legal deadline while waiting for rulemaking.

Sources: Florida DFS hearing notice, Florida Administrative Code

5. Afternoon storms can cause localized street flooding

Local 10 forecasts a stronger thunderstorm window from 2 p.m. to 6 p.m. Stronger cells could drop a couple of inches quickly and flood poor-drainage streets; the heat index approaches 105 before storms. NWS Miami's point forecast called for likely storms mainly from 3 p.m. to 5 p.m. and a heat index as high as 106.

Action: protect open roofs, interiors, materials, and drains before the afternoon; use heat and lightning stops; never drive through flooded streets.

Sources: Local 10, National Weather Service Miami

SEGMENT NOTES

  • Homeowners: - With an existing My Safe Florida Home inspection, check grant eligibility and timing now. - Recalculate mortgage and total housing cost before changing a purchase budget. - Protect active repairs and property records before afternoon storms.
  • Service Providers: - Do not guarantee My Safe Florida Home reimbursement. - Work from Miami-Dade's final RFA when supporting a multifamily applicant. - Secure and photograph open work, and maintain heat and lightning protocols.
  • Real Estate Professionals: - Refresh payment scenarios with current lender quotes. - Verify program status from actual My Safe Florida Home records. - Put the August 14 Miami-Dade multifamily deadline on the capital calendar.
